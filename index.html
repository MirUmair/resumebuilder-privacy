Offline-first privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ResumeFlow handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Privacy at a glance ResumeFlow is designed to work offline. We do not require an account, show advertisements, use analytics or tracking tools, or upload your resumes, cover letters, job descriptions, templates, or other CV data to our servers.

1. Information You Enter Into the App

You may choose to enter information into the app in order to create, edit, analyze, save, import, export, or manage resumes and cover letters.

This information may include:

  • Name, job title, email address, phone number, and location
  • Profile photo
  • Professional summaries and career objectives
  • Work experience and achievements
  • Education history
  • Skills and technologies
  • Projects
  • Certifications and credentials
  • Languages and proficiency levels
  • Awards, volunteering, courses, publications, training, interests, and references
  • Job descriptions that you paste into the app for local ATS or keyword matching
  • Cover-letter content
  • Custom CV templates, images, PDFs, and element mappings
  • Resume data imported from JSON files
  • Backup files that you choose to create or restore

This information is used only to provide the features you request inside the app.

2. Local Processing and Storage

Your resume data, cover letters, job descriptions, custom templates, app settings, and related information are processed locally on your device.

The app automatically saves resume drafts and other supported content locally so that you can continue your work later.

We do not operate a remote account system or cloud database that stores your CV content, and we do not receive the information you enter into your resumes.

3. Custom CV Template Cloning

The app allows you to select JPG, JPEG, PNG, or PDF files and use them as custom CV templates.

Template previewing, PDF first-page rendering, field mapping, editing, resizing, positioning, and template saving are performed on your device.

Custom templates and CV files are not sent to an external artificial intelligence service or uploaded to our servers.

4. Offline Writing Assistance

Features such as summary drafting, achievement-writing assistance, profession-based skill suggestions, cover-letter drafting, ATS scoring, resume recommendations, and job-description keyword matching operate locally.

The app does not send your resume or job-description content to an external AI provider for these features.

5. ATS Scores and Job Matching

ATS scores, resume completion scores, strengths, recommendations, keyword matches, and potentially missing keywords are estimates generated locally by the app.

Job descriptions that you paste into the app remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share related content yourself.

6. Resume and Cover-Letter Exports

The app can generate or export files such as PDF resumes, editable DOCX resumes, JSON resume data, cover-letter PDFs, and backup files.

Exported files are saved or shared only when you choose an export, save, or share action.

If you choose to share a file using another app or service, such as email, messaging, cloud storage, or a file-sharing provider, that third party's privacy practices will apply after the file leaves ResumeFlow.

7. Import and Backup Features

You may import validated resume JSON files or restore a local backup. Imported data is processed and stored locally.

Backup files are created only when you choose to create a backup. You are responsible for where you save or share those backup files.

8. Device Permissions

The app may request access to files, documents, photos, or media when required for a feature you initiate, such as:

  • Selecting a profile photo
  • Importing an image or PDF template
  • Importing resume JSON or backup files
  • Saving or exporting a resume, cover letter, or backup

These permissions are used only to perform the requested local function. We do not use these permissions to silently upload your personal files.

9. Accounts

ResumeFlow does not require account registration or sign-in.

Because there is no app account, we do not collect usernames, passwords, or account-profile information for operating the app.

10. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

ResumeFlow does not display advertisements and does not use analytics or tracking tools to monitor your behavior across apps or websites.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your resume information or personal data.

11. Data Sharing

We do not share your resume content, cover letters, job descriptions, custom templates, or other locally stored CV information with third parties because this information is not transmitted to us.

Data may leave the app only when you deliberately use a device feature to export, save, or share a file with another application or service.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Data created in ResumeFlow remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove the relevant files.

The app provides tools to delete resumes, drafts, cover letters, cloned template files, and other locally stored app content.

You may also use the app's option to delete all locally stored resume data and supported files.

Because we do not maintain server-side copies of your CV information, we cannot restore locally deleted information for you.

13. Security

We design the app to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information by keeping resume-related processing on your device.

However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. You should protect your device using appropriate security features such as a PIN, password, fingerprint, face authentication, or other device security options.

You should also take care when exporting or sharing resumes because resumes commonly contain personal contact and employment information.

14. Children's Privacy

ResumeFlow is a productivity tool for creating resumes, CVs, and related career documents.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our servers because resume and CV information is processed locally and is not transmitted to us.

15. Third-Party Apps and Services

When you choose to open, save, or share exported content using another app or service, your interaction with that third party is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

ResumeFlow is not responsible for how third-party applications handle information that you intentionally send to them.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, privacy practices, or legal requirements change.

When we update this page, we will revise the effective date shown at the top of the policy.
